# Student research log

Create one dated Markdown entry per substantive work session. This log is for learning, scientific memory, and honest authorship—not performance.

```markdown
# YYYY-MM-DD — short title

## Question I worked on

## What I predicted, and why

## What I did myself

## AI/tool assistance used

## Evidence created (paths, figures, outputs)

## What surprised me / what failed

## What I now believe, with confidence level

## Next smallest test
```

For a major decision, also update `docs/decision-log.md`. Do not rewrite the log later to make the process look cleaner than it was.
