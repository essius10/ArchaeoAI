# 2026-08-28 — E001 bounded real-terrain pilot

## Question I worked on

Can approved E001 locations be reconstructed privately and converted through a bounded Environment
Agency WCS request into deterministic, validated 128 m terrain patches without exposing locations or
starting machine learning?

## What I predicted, and why

I expected 128 m to preserve useful mound and ditch context for most accepted bowl barrows while
remaining small enough to limit landscape signal and transfer size. I expected the persistent EA WCS
to be safer and much smaller than downloading all intersected 5 km tiles.

## What I did myself

The student approved Phase 2B, the target class, privacy boundary, pilot-first sequence, and explicit
prohibition on machine learning. No additional student-authored analysis is recorded in this
automated session.

## AI/tool assistance used

Codex audited the existing evidence, checked official wheel metadata and EA service metadata, added
and tested the raster modules, reconstructed locations through the official NHLE service into
ignored storage, ran the one-site then five-site pilot, visually reviewed private QA mosaics, and
calculated the coordinate-safe acquisition estimate. No sub-agent was used.

## What surprised me / what failed

The full-tile alternative would touch 200 distinct 5 km cells—about 20 GB uncompressed—while the
bounded WCS strategy projects only about 17.2 MB of raw responses. Five of five pilot requests passed
with zero no-data. Visual QA also showed forestry striping, linear features, and isolated strong
local objects, demonstrating that interpretable transforms still preserve non-archaeological
confounds.

One initial PowerShell `DescribeCoverage` command built an invalid local URI because the base variable
was not braced before `?`; it made no request. The corrected request succeeded. An intentionally
non-georeferenced QA PNG produced an expected Rasterio warning, which is now narrowly suppressed.
An idempotent cache-only rerun briefly made the runtime estimate too optimistic; the runner gained a
bounded `--refresh` mode, and a five-response refresh preserved every checksum. No pilot download
failed.

## What I now believe, with confidence level

High confidence: the bounded WCS and raster-validation design can safely acquire E001 positive
patches at student scale. Moderate confidence: 128 m is an appropriate primary context, because it is
supported by description-derived diameters and the pipeline remains configurable. Low confidence:
that all 261 will pass pixel QA, because only five are pixel-verified and the service may vary.
