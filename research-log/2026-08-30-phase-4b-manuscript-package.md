## Scope

Converted the frozen E001 evidence into a coordinate-safe academic manuscript draft and a future
release/reproducibility package. No model was loaded, trained, tuned, or scored; no terrain or
labels were acquired; and no public release, preprint, DOI, or deployment was executed.

## Outputs

- A 4,000–7,000-word manuscript presenting the E001, robustness, compact-CNN, external-validation,
  and post-hoc error-analysis evidence without pooling their distinct results.
- Formal Data Availability and Code Availability statements.
- Reproducibility, release-checklist, release-plan, and citation-audit documents.
- A machine-readable evidence manifest binding the manuscript and seven coordinate-safe figures to
  the immutable Phase 3C, external-dataset, prediction-vector, Phase 4A, model, and RF-configuration
  hashes.
- Automated checks for exact metrics, spent-test and exploratory boundaries, prohibited claims,
  coordinate safety, no-training behavior, and evidence mutation.

## Scientific boundary

The headline external result remains the one-time Phase 3C result. The external test remains spent,
and Phase 4A remains `POST-HOC / EXPLORATORY`. The Phase 2F review packet remains unreviewed. The
package makes no archaeological-discovery claim and does not represent peer review or publication.
