## What surprised me / what failed

The random and geographic final assignments overlap alternative conditions rather than forming one
universal hidden set. To keep this run bounded, only the primary geographic condition was evaluated.
A Ruff import-order check initially failed before the result commit; it was corrected and all checks
were rerun. Scikit-learn 1.9 emits a future deprecation warning for the explicitly pre-registered
`penalty="l2"` spelling; the equivalent committed model behavior was preserved rather than silently
changing the post-score specification.

The selected all-four Random Forest reached 0.821429 balanced accuracy on only 28 development
observations. One of five permuted-label runs reached 0.714286, illustrating the instability of a
small development set; the permutation mean was 0.528571.

## What I now believe, with confidence level

High confidence: the code enforces the active final-test boundary, feature arrays contain no
metadata, the 15-candidate matrix follows the committed rule, and the frozen configuration hash is
valid.

Low-to-moderate confidence: the selected model may generalize geographically. Development evidence
is encouraging but too small to support that claim before the untouched final test.
