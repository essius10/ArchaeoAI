## Diagnostics and correction

The two geographic blocks each contributed four errors. Provenance/year and group counts remained
class-balanced; resolution was uniformly 1 m; paths, filenames, metadata, and raw absolute
elevation were absent from model features. All final masks contain zero missing cells.

Post-result review found that the descriptive audit initially wrote `1 - mean(mask)` even though
the stored mask uses `True` for missing cells. This made zero-missing patches appear fully missing.
The audit field and source expression were corrected to `mean(mask)` without rerunning a model or
changing any prediction, metric, interval, or scientific conclusion.

Hard-relief, forestry, and road/track flags do not exist consistently for every final observation;
they were not inferred after seeing errors.
