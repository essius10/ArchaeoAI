# 2026-08-28 — E001 curated-data gate

## Question I worked on

Can a deterministic, geographically broad subset of the NHLE bowl-barrow candidate pool yield at
least 250 records that survive official-entry, geometry, terrain-coverage, and provenance checks?

## What I predicted, and why

The Phase 2A 30-entry sample suggested the full-entry yield might be high, but it could not predict
geometry or terrain attrition. The gate therefore reviewed 360 records without assuming that the
sample acceptance rate would hold.

## What I did myself

The student approved the Phase 2A.5 question, quality thresholds, privacy rules, and prohibition on
starting raster or model work. No additional student-authored analysis is recorded in this automated
session.

## AI/tool assistance used

Codex created and tested the deterministic rubric and runner, loaded all 360 official Historic
England entries, queried official NHLE and EA metadata services, checked the coordinate-free
aggregates, and drafted the decision record. This was one primary review session, not an independent
human review.

## What surprised me / what failed

An initial six-worker terrain-index run triggered 227 metadata-query errors. Those records were
correctly left unresolved. Reducing the query to the Composite index's own source-provenance field,
adding bounded retries, and rerunning with two workers produced zero query errors. Forty-seven sites
crossed more than one survey-provenance extent and were withheld rather than forced into the clean
pool.

## What I now believe, with confidence level

High confidence: the source strategy can supply a student-scale E001 pool. The final clean count is
261 across 23 coarse groups, including 12 groups with at least 12 records. Moderate confidence: the
labels are reliable enough to begin controlled Phase 2B design. That confidence cannot become high
until a different human reviews the frozen 40-record reliability queue and backgrounds are matched
by acquisition provenance.

## Next smallest test

Have an independent human apply the frozen rubric to the 40-record queue, then design a private,
manifested Phase 2B pilot that tests matched-background availability without downloading national
terrain or training a model.
