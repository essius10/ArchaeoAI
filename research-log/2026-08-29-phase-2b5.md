# 2026-08-29 — E001 full positive-terrain freeze

## Question I worked on

Can the five-site Phase 2B pilot be extended to every approved positive record while retaining
bounded access, deterministic processing, provenance, overlap constraints, and a strict private
coordinate boundary?

## What I predicted, and why

The pilot projected about 17.2 MB of raw WCS responses and predicted eight cross-cell patches. I
expected most or all of the 256 remaining windows to pass because all 261 had already passed
metadata-level coverage checks, but I treated service errors, corrupt responses, no-data, and
representation failures as real possible rejection outcomes.

## What surprised me / what failed

No WCS request failed or retried. The 256 new responses plus five reused caches all passed. The
eight predicted cross-cell cases were exact: no duplicate boundary row/column or exceptional seam
step appeared. Raw storage matched the 17,214,255-byte estimate exactly; compressed processed
storage was 1,718,765 bytes below estimate. Controlled two-worker acquisition took 190.919 seconds,
much less than the 702-second sequential pilot projection.

Private visual QA found frequent non-archaeological terrain confounds—modern linear features,
forestry or terrain striping, roads/tracks, a building/structure, steep terrain, and high-contrast
local objects—but no technically invalid panel. The Codex usage limit interrupted the session after
acquisition and initial audit generation. Resumption preserved the cache and uncommitted work,
revalidated every artifact without contacting the WCS, and completed the visual review.

## What I now believe, with confidence level

High confidence: the private E001 positive-terrain dataset is complete and internally reproducible
on the current CPython 3.14.7 environment. High confidence: overlap groups and acquisition
provenance are explicit enough to constrain the next design phase. Moderate confidence: future
background matching can control the observed terrain confounds; that has not been tested. No
confidence claim is made about classification or geographic generalization because no background,
split, or model exists.
