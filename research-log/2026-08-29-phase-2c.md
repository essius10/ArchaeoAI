# 2026-08-29 — Phase 2C background and split freeze

## Question I worked on

Can E001 form a balanced, provenance-controlled dataset and compare random with geographic
evaluation without treating unknown terrain as true negative evidence or leaking related patches?

## What I predicted, and why

I expected a 1:1 matched design to be feasible because every positive had local EA Composite
coverage metadata. I expected provenance edges and missing terrain to cause many candidate
rejections, and I expected hard modern/forestry terrain to appear in realistic backgrounds.

## What surprised me / what failed

The first 10-record command stopped before terrain download because the metadata field is named
`resolution_m`, not `resolution`. The one-line fix passed tests and the private resumable state
continued safely. The full sampler required 573 candidates: provenance mismatch was the largest
rejection category (156), followed by group boundaries (64), Scheduled Monument exclusions (46),
missing terrain (40), and positive buffers (six). A deterministic 25-record visual sample contained
substantial realistic hard confounds, which were retained.

The descriptive audit also found that positive patches have higher raw median elevation than the
backgrounds. Raw elevation is not a frozen model representation—elevation is median-normalized per
patch—but the imbalance is recorded rather than hidden.

The first final audit checked window overlap but did not encode the requested geographic separation
buffer. Before modelling, a private distance audit showed the frozen assignment already exceeded a
1 km cross-partition centre buffer. That 1 km invariant was then added without changing any sample
or assignment; only the zero-violation aggregate is tracked.

## What I now believe, with confidence level

High confidence: the tracked dataset is coordinate-safe, class-balanced, deterministic, exactly
matched on coarse geography/provenance/year, and free of the audited hard leakage conditions.

Moderate confidence: the design is suitable for bounded baseline modelling. It cannot prove that
backgrounds contain no archaeology, fully eliminate local landscape confounding, or support strong
public claims before independent label review and CPython 3.12 reproduction.
