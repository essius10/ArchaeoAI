## Evidence-state rules

- `PROFESSIONAL_FEEDBACK` records a participant’s workflow observation; it is not customer intent.
- `CUSTOMER_INTEREST` requires a relevant organization to request continued evaluation.
- `PILOT_INTEREST` requires a credible, conditional path to a defined pilot; it is not approval.
- `PAID_WILLINGNESS` requires a concrete procurement or budget signal tied to a defined offer; it
  is not revenue.
- `PILOT_COMMITMENT` requires documented organizational authorization and scope outside this
  register; none exists.

Interview summaries must separate what the participant said from interviewer interpretation.
Attributable quotations require permission. Anonymous entries should retain enough private source
evidence for audit without making the participant re-identifiable in public material.
