## Decision rule

All required gates must be satisfied or have an explicit, accountable `GO WITH CONDITIONS` decision
that is permissible under Phase 6A. A `NO-GO`, unknown legal/data/model authority, incomplete Phase
5E finding relevant to the deployment, absent qualified reviewer, or missing privacy/deletion plan
is a hard stop. A high ICP score, positive interview, or pilot interest never substitutes for these
conditions.
