# Product discovery and pilot success metrics

## Measurement rule

These are candidate measures for a future separately authorized pilot, not observed results or
performance promises. Baselines, units, collection methods, acceptable ranges, stop conditions,
sample size, and decision rules must be agreed with the professional pilot partner **before** any
real processing. Do not optimize the model or reinterpret the spent external test from pilot data.

## Workflow measures

| Measure | Proposed definition | Evidence required | Interpretation caution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Terrain workload | Authorized area and number of canonical patches admitted/rejected. | Processing receipt and input contract. | Area alone does not show value. |
| Baseline professional review time | Time for the agreed current workflow on comparable in-scope work. | Participant-approved method or observed baseline. | Self-report and task differences must be disclosed. |
| Assisted professional review time | Active qualified-review time using the bounded workflow. | Auditable session/task records with consent. | Do not count unattended compute as professional time saved. |
| Proportion prioritized | Predefined fraction of admitted terrain entering focused review. | Frozen prioritization rule and aggregate counts. | Lower is not automatically better; missed-feature risk matters. |
| Review completion burden | Time, number of items, and unresolved items per reviewer. | Review records. | Expertise and case complexity affect comparisons. |

## Usefulness and quality measures

| Measure | Proposed definition | Evidence required | Interpretation caution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Useful-prioritization rate | Fraction of reviewed AI hypotheses that qualified reviewers judge useful for the stated workflow under pre-agreed categories. | Attributed review and category definitions. | Not archaeological accuracy or discovery rate. |
| False-positive burden | Fraction/count of prioritized items judged non-useful, obvious modern/natural confounds, or insufficient evidence. | Human review record with denominator. | `unlabelled_background` is not a known negative. |
| Reviewer agreement | Agreement on controlled morphology/workflow categories for independently reviewed overlap items. | Blinded assignment and predefined statistic. | Agreement does not confirm archaeology. |
| Missed-feature concern | Reviewer-flagged potentially relevant terrain outside the prioritized set under an approved sampling audit. | Pre-agreed safe audit design and human review. | Cannot claim absence or completeness from limited review. |
| Workflow satisfaction | Structured ratings plus qualitative reasons about fit, trust, and burden. | Participant feedback instrument. | Satisfaction is not payment or professional validation. |
| Report usefulness | Whether the bounded report supports the intended professional handoff and records limits clearly. | Reviewer/client assessment against predefined criteria. | Does not imply planning/legal acceptance. |

## Reliability and governance measures

| Measure | Proposed definition | Evidence required | Stop concern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Input validation reliability | Valid inputs accepted and invalid/ambiguous inputs rejected by controlled reasons. | Test and pilot aggregate; no private values. | Unsafe acceptance or silent repair. |
| Processing completion | Authorized jobs completed within agreed bounded resources. | Audited job states and resource summaries. | Repeated failure, unbounded resource use, or hidden retries. |
| Evidence-boundary compliance | Outputs/reports preserve AI versus human levels and controlled terminology. | Output audit. | Any automatic promotion or archaeological certainty claim. |
| Privacy incidents | Unauthorized access, sensitive logging, retention, export, or location exposure. | Incident and access audit. | Any material exposure triggers stop and response. |
| Deletion verification | Eligible sensitive objects and derivatives removed by deadline across stores/backups. | Deletion receipt and restore-path test. | Unverifiable or incomplete deletion. |
| Professional override/escalation | Frequency and reasons humans reject, defer, or escalate AI hypotheses. | Evidence history. | Overrides are learning evidence, not model failure to conceal. |

## Commercial signals—kept separate

Record continuation requests, credible pilot expansion requests, procurement steps, budget-owner
involvement, and concrete conditional payment signals separately from workflow metrics. None is
revenue until an agreement is executed and payment occurs. Do not invent ROI or state that
ArchaeoAI saves any percentage of time without a valid measured comparison.

## Pilot decision output

A future pilot report should state measures and denominators, uncertainty and missing data,
baseline comparability, supporting and contrary feedback, safety events, and whether pre-agreed
criteria were met. The owner and professional partner must decide continuation; a single composite
“success score” must not hide scientific, privacy, or professional blockers.
