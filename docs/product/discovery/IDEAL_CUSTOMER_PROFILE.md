# Ideal customer profile scorecard

## Primary hypothesis

The first discovery priority is an archaeological consultancy, or an archaeology/heritage team
inside a multidisciplinary environmental consultancy, that regularly reviews substantial terrain,
already uses LiDAR/DTM/GIS, retains qualified archaeological judgement, and can evaluate a bounded
private pilot. This is a targeting hypothesis, not a claim that such organizations will buy.

## Scoring method

Score each dimension from public evidence or a documented conversation:

- `0` — absent, contradicted, or unknown;
- `1` — plausible or occasional, but weakly evidenced;
- `2` — directly and specifically evidenced.

Unknown is scored `0`, not inferred. Record the evidence source and date beside the score. The
maximum is 20; a number ranks discovery suitability only and does not predict purchase.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regularly reviews archaeological terrain | None/unknown | Occasional | Recurring core workflow |
| Handles LiDAR/DTM/GIS | None/unknown | Indirect or occasional | Routine direct use |
| Handles sufficiently large sites | Small/unknown | Sometimes large | Repeated large-area work |
| Manual review burden | None/unknown | Some friction | Specific recurring burden |
| Repeat project volume | One-off/unknown | Intermittent | Recurrent comparable projects |
| Professional archaeology expertise | None/unknown | External/limited | Qualified expertise in team |
| Ability to test software | No/unknown | Constrained | Has relevant people/data/environment |
| Ability to authorize a pilot | No/unknown | Can influence | Has credible approval path |
| Privacy/security fit | Incompatible/unknown | Requirements unclear | Requirements articulated and potentially compatible |
| Budget authority or influence | None/unknown | Influencer | Budget/procurement path evidenced |

## Tiers

- **Tier 1 — best discovery target (16–20):** strong workflow fit, relevant professional expertise,
  and credible evaluation influence. Prioritize for a respectful interview request.
- **Tier 2 — useful secondary target (10–15):** meaningful learning value but incomplete workflow,
  authority, scale, or evidence. Include selectively to test assumptions.
- **Tier 3 — low priority (0–9):** weak or unknown fit. Do not spend early outreach capacity unless
  the prospect fills a deliberate sample gap.

Regardless of score, exclude an organization from pilot consideration if it cannot establish data
authority, qualified human review, privacy compatibility, or an accountable project owner. A high
ICP score is not pilot approval.

## Secondary groups

Independent archaeology/GIS specialists can provide high-quality workflow evidence even if their
buying path is limited. Infrastructure, renewables, and planning heritage teams may illuminate
commissioning and governance, but discovery must not position software as a substitute for
archaeological consultants or professional decisions.
