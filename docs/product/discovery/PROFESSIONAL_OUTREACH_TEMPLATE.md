# Professional outreach template

Use individual, relevant public professional contact routes only. Do not scrape private contact
details, mass-message, imply endorsement, or send outreach without owner review.

## Template

**Subject:** Student research on professional LiDAR terrain-review workflows

Hello [Name],

I’m developing ArchaeoAI, an independent student-led research project examining bounded machine-
learning support for LiDAR-derived archaeological terrain review. It does not claim to discover or
confirm archaeology, and I’m not contacting you with a sales offer.

where the difficult or repetitive work occurs, and what evidence, privacy, and human-review

Would you be open to a 15–20 minute conversation about your current workflow? There is no
obligation to test or buy anything, and feedback can be anonymized if preferred.

Thank you,

[Owner name]
[Student-led ArchaeoAI research link]

## Personalization check

Before sending, confirm the recipient’s role is relevant, state why their public professional work
made the request appropriate, avoid claims about their organization, and provide a simple way to
decline further contact.
