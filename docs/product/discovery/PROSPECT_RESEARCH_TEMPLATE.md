## Research method

1. Define the intended mix of Groups A–D before searching.
2. Prefer official organization pages, professional directories, and clearly public contact routes.
3. Record the exact public evidence supporting each ICP score; unknown remains unknown.
4. Separate organizational capability from assumptions about a particular person or buyer.
5. Deduplicate organizations and avoid contacting multiple people indiscriminately.
6. Owner-review Tier 1/2 relevance and message personalization before any outreach.
7. Record contact, response, interview, and pilot states factually; silence is not rejection and a
   conversation is not customer or pilot commitment.
