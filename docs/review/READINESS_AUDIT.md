# Phase 4C independent-review readiness audit

## Decision

**READY_FOR_EXTERNAL_REVIEW**

This means the coordinate-safe E001 manuscript package is suitable to send to independent academic
reviewers for critical feedback. It does not mean peer reviewed, publication-ready, licensed for
reuse, or approved by an institution.

## Audit outcome

- Frozen Phase 3C, Phase 4A, dataset, prediction, Random-Forest state, and configuration bindings
  remain unchanged; the external test remains spent.
- The manuscript's headline is preserved: **84.2% balanced accuracy, 95% paired-bootstrap CI
  77.5–90.0%, n = 120, across five pre-specified external geographic cells**.
- All references currently used in the manuscript have verified identities and claim fit. The
  previously incomplete Character et al. record is resolved, and verified methods references now
  support spatial validation, balanced accuracy, and clustered bootstrap methodology.
- Confirmatory, post-hoc, and exploratory boundaries are explicit. The manuscript makes no causal,
  archaeological-discovery, calibrated-probability, field-validation, or England-wide claim.
- Public reproduction is classified as **PARTIALLY REPRODUCIBLE — PRIVATE DATA REQUIRED FOR
  SPECIFIC STEPS**. Coordinate-safe evidence validates without private data.
- Public manuscript, evidence, reviewer files, and SVG figures contain no exact coordinate, NGR,
  heritage ID, candidate row, sensitive map, or private filesystem path. SVG metadata is limited to
  non-location technical provenance.
- Provider attributions and OGL obligations are documented, but repository ownership and split
  licensing remain unresolved release blockers.

## Remaining blockers before publication or release

1. Complete a systematic literature search and obtain subject-expert review of omissions and
   novelty framing.
2. Obtain independent archaeological, spatial-methods, statistical, reproducibility, and privacy
   review; record dispositions rather than implying agreement.
3. Complete or explicitly report the outstanding 40-record independent label-reliability review.
4. Resolve repository copyright ownership, file-class licensing, and the Historic England source-
   item attribution wording discrepancy; do not add a blanket licence prematurely.
5. Obtain legal/terms review before redistributing any third-party-derived row-level dataset.
6. Conduct a controlled independent full-data rerun where sensitive-data access is lawful, and add
   an archival dependency specification.
7. Keep the Phase 2F candidate packet private and unreviewed until its separate blinded-review gate.

None of these blocks requesting external feedback. They do block describing the package as
publication-ready or executing a public release.
